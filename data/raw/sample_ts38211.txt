4.2 Numerologies
Multiple OFDM numerologies are supported as given by Table 4.2-1.
Table 4.2-1: Supported transmission numerologies
mu    Subcarrier spacing (kHz)   Cyclic prefix
0     15                         Normal
1     30                         Normal
2     60                         Normal, Extended
3     120                        Normal
4     240                        Normal
5     480                        Normal
6     960                        Normal
Only the numerology mu=2 with subcarrier spacing 60 kHz supports
extended cyclic prefix. Other numerologies use normal cyclic prefix only.

4.3 Frame structure
4.3.1 Frames and subframes
Downlink, uplink, and sidelink transmissions are organized into frames.

4.3.1 Cyclic prefix
For normal cyclic prefix, the cyclic prefix length T_CP is given by
T_CP = 144 * kappa * 2^(-mu) * T_c
where kappa = 64 and T_c = 1 / (480000 * 4096) seconds approximately 0.509 ns.
